# Changelog

Short notes for each release. The app shows them when it updates.

## 1.2.0

- **Talk both ways.** Live Translate now translates the other side too: they hear you in their language, and you read them in yours.
- **Hush AI Speaker.** Choose it as the speaker in your meeting app and Hush cleans their noisy voice before you hear it. Voice-over plays the translation over their voice, like an interpreter.
- **Conversations.** Every call is kept as a conversation, grouped by meeting, with search, stars, filters and export.
- **The notch.** Hush sits at the top of your screen, wakes up in calls and opens when you point at it. Prefer the tray widget? It is one setting away.
- **Privacy.** Hide Hush from screenshots, screen sharing and recordings.
- **Calendar filter** in Meetings, and tray icons that are easy to see on light and dark bars.

## 1.1.0

- **DeepFilterNet 3** is the new default engine: full band, one CPU thread. NVIDIA RE-USE stays one click away.
- **Studio** cleans recordings and videos you drop on it, or a recording you make.
- **Meetings** follows your Google Calendars and turns Hush on before calls.
- **Live Translate** (Preview): speak your language and the call hears theirs.

## 1.0.0

- First public release: real-time noise cancellation with NVIDIA RE-USE and Hush AI Microphone.
